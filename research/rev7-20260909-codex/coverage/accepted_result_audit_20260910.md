# ASTRA accepted-result audit (2026-09-10)

These are nine representative results, not an inventory of every attempted model. A finite exclusion remains conditional on its input, cipher, representation and endpoint assumptions. Positive controls check implementation behavior; they do not convert language rankings into proofs. Completed searches were not rerun for this audit.
